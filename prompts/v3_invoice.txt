Extract the invoice below into JSON. Follow these rules for each field:
- invoice_number: the invoice number exactly as printed, without labels, brackets or "#".
- vendor: the name of the business that issued the invoice. customer: the name of the business being billed. Names only, no addresses.
- invoice_date / due_date: YYYY-MM-DD.
- currency: the 3-letter ISO code only (USD, EUR, GBP, ...).
- subtotal, discount, tax, total: plain numbers. discount is positive. tax is the money amount, not the rate. Use 0 when absent.
- line_items: EVERY row, in order, from all pages. Never skip or invent rows.

Example input:
RECHNUNG / INVOICE  Müller GmbH
Kunde / Customer: Hansen AG
Rechnungsnr.: RE-2026-00042   Rechnungsdatum: 03.02.2026   Fällig am: 17.02.2026
1  Laptop stand  2  1.249,00 €  2.498,00 €
2  Setup (Week 1)  3  85,00 €  255,00 €
Zwischensumme / Subtotal  2.753,00 €
Rabatt / Discount  -53,00 €
MwSt. / VAT 19%  513,00 €
Gesamtbetrag / Total  3.213,00 €

Example output:
{"invoice_number": "RE-2026-00042", "vendor": "Müller GmbH", "customer": "Hansen AG",
 "invoice_date": "2026-02-03", "due_date": "2026-02-17", "currency": "EUR",
 "subtotal": 2753.0, "discount": 53.0, "tax": 513.0, "total": 3213.0,
 "line_items": [
  {"description": "Laptop stand", "quantity": 2, "unit_price": 1249.0, "amount": 2498.0},
  {"description": "Setup (Week 1)", "quantity": 3, "unit_price": 85.0, "amount": 255.0}]}

Before answering, check your work: the line item amounts must add up to subtotal, and subtotal - discount + tax must equal total. If they don't, you missed or misread a row, so look again.
