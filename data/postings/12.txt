About us
Our mission at Credal is to harness frontier AI for everyone building a better future.

We accelerate the most complex, sophisticated operations of the most impactful institutions, while weaving security and governance into every part of our product. Our customers include the U.S. Dept of Health and Human Services, MongoDB, Comcast NBC Universal, Lattice, Wise (Transferwise), Checkr, and incident.io among others.

We’ve raised over $10m to date with a ton of runway. You’ll join a team of Thoughtful Doers from places like Palantir, Cruise, Meta, Uber, and Google.

Credal is in-person in our spacious NYC office with meals provided.

About the role
We’re looking for a founding web full-stack software engineer to help us build on our progress, with proven market demand from global enterprises, to a scalable, performant architecture while maintaining our industry leading security posture.

What you’ll do:

Own aspects of our stability, scalability and security as we onboard more customers and expand our product

Ensure we can successfully sync billions of records into our data platform from our customers’ SaaS tools reliably

Contribute to architecture, design decisions, and code and product quality strategy

Engage directly with customers and our GTM team to discover and solve our customers’ most complex and bleeding edge problems

Mentor junior engineers and continue raising our bar for engineering excellence

Take end-to-end ownership of our core products, built on…

Frontend: React, Typescript, NextJS, Tailwind

Backend: Typescript, Temporal, NextJS, Gel/PostgreSQL

AI/ML: OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Gemini, Milvus/QDrant/MongoDB

Cloud: AWS, Terraform, Helm, Kubernetes
